## Variants

Some products come in multiple versions — like sizes or flavors (e.g. a drink available in 200ml, 300ml, 500ml, or 1L). These are called **variants**.

1. Tap the product (e.g. "Pepsi").
2. A variant list appears — tap the one the guest wants (e.g. "200ml").
3. If that variant has add-ons available, you'll move to the modifier screen next.

<Tip>
  Each variant is priced and configured independently — a size that requires manual price entry won't affect other sizes of the same product.
</Tip>

## Modifiers

Modifiers are add-ons or customizations for an item — like "extra cheese" or "no onions."

1. After selecting the product (or variant), the modifier screen appears if any are available.
2. Tap to select the modifiers the guest wants.
3. Some modifier groups require a minimum or maximum number of selections — the screen will guide you.
4. Tap **Confirm** to add the item with its modifiers to the cart.

## Reviewing modifiers in the cart

Items with modifiers show them listed underneath the item name in the cart, so both you and the kitchen can see exactly what was customized.
