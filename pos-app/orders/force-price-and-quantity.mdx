title: "Force Price & Force Quantity"
description: "When the app asks you to manually enter a price or quantity"
---

## What this means

Some products are set up to require the cashier to manually enter a **price**, a **quantity**, or both, before the item can be added to the cart. This is common for items like:

- Weighed goods (priced per gram/kg)
- Custom or negotiable-price items
- Bulk items sold in variable quantities

## What you'll see

When you tap an item configured this way, a prompt appears asking you to enter the required value(s) before it's added:

1. Enter the **quantity** if required.
2. Enter the **price** if required.
3. Tap **Confirm** to add the item.

<Note>
  If a product has variants (like different sizes), this prompt appears **per variant** — each size may have its own independent setting, so you might be asked again when picking a specific variant even after entering a price for the general product.
</Note>

## If you can't remember the correct price

Check with your manager or the printed price list before entering a value — incorrect manual pricing will affect reporting and reconciliation.
