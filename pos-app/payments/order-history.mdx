## Viewing orders

Tap **Orders** in the side menu to see a full list of orders, filterable by:

- **All** — every order
- **Active** — currently open, unsettled orders
- **Completed** — fully paid orders
- **Transferred** — orders moved between tables or devices
- **Sync Failed** — orders that haven't synced to the cloud yet (see [Common Issues](/pos-app/troubleshooting/common-issues))

## Reading an order card

Each order shows:

- Order number and status
- Which device and staff member created it
- Date and time
- Itemized list with subtotal, tax, and total
- Payment method, if settled

## Searching

Use the employee filter at the top to narrow results to a specific staff member, or use **Refresh** to pull the latest orders if you're expecting one that isn't showing yet.
