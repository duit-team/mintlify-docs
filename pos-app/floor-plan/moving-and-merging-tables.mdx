---
title: "Moving & Merging Tables"
description: "Move a whole table, move individual items, or merge tickets"
---

## Moving a whole table

Use this when a guest wants to switch tables entirely.

1. Open the table you want to move.
2. Tap **Move** at the top of the cart.
3. Select the destination table.
4. Confirm.

<Note>
  If the destination table has less seating capacity than your current guest count, you'll see a warning — you can still confirm if extra seating is available at the new spot.
</Note>

## Moving specific items

Use this when only some items (not the whole table) need to move — for example, splitting a group across two tables.

1. Open the table.
2. Tap **Move Items**.
3. Select which items should move (you don't have to move everything).
4. Choose the destination — an existing bill, a new bill on another table, or an empty table.
5. Confirm.

<Tip>
  Only items that were already sent to the kitchen will show up on the kitchen ticket at the new table. Draft items you haven't sent yet move quietly and still need to be sent from the new table.
</Tip>

## Merging tickets

If two separate tables are joining into one bill:

1. Open one of the tables.
2. Tap **Merge**.
3. Select the other table/bill to merge in.
4. Confirm.
