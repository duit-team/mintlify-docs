---
title: "Logging In"
description: "How to sign in to DuitPOS and start your shift"
---

## Signing in

1. Open the DuitPOS app on your device.
2. Select your **role** (POS, Kiosk, or Kitchen Display) if prompted.
3. Enter your **email/username** and **PIN or password**.
4. Tap **Sign In**.

<Tip>
  If this is the first time the app is opening on this device, it may take a minute to sync menu items, tables, and settings from the cloud. Keep the device connected to Wi-Fi during this step.
</Tip>

## Starting your shift

Once signed in, you'll usually need to **start your shift** before taking orders:

1. Tap **Shift** in the side menu.
2. Tap **Start Shift**.
3. Enter your opening cash drawer amount if your location requires it.
4. Tap **Confirm**.

You'll see a green **Shift ON** badge once your shift is active.

## Switching users

If multiple staff share the same device:

1. Tap **Switch** in the bottom-left menu.
2. Select your name or enter your PIN.

<Warning>
  Always end your shift before handing the device to another staff member — this keeps cash reconciliation accurate for each person.
</Warning>

## Ending your shift

1. Tap **End Shift**.
2. Confirm your closing cash amount.
3. Review the shift summary and tap **Confirm**.

<Note>
  Once a shift is ended, you'll need to log in and start a new shift to take further orders.
</Note>
