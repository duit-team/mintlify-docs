---
title: "Barcode Scanning"
description: "Scan or type a barcode from anywhere in the app"
---

DuitPOS listens for barcode input **in the background**, from anywhere in the app — you don't need to click into a search box first.

## How it works

- A connected barcode scanner can be aimed and triggered at any time, on any screen, and the app will pick up the scan automatically.
- If you don't have a physical scanner, you can **manually type the barcode digits** on your keyboard followed by **Enter**, and the app treats it exactly like a scan — again, without needing to click into any specific field first.

<Tip>
  This works even if your cursor or focus is somewhere else on the screen — you don't need to be inside a search bar or any input field for a scan (or typed + Enter) to register.
</Tip>

## What happens when a barcode is scanned

- If the barcode matches a known product, that item is added to the current cart automatically.
- If the product has variants or modifiers, you may be prompted to choose them, same as tapping the item manually.
- If no match is found, the app will show a "not found" message rather than silently doing nothing.

## When this is useful

- Quickly ringing up packaged items without searching the menu grid
- Speeding up checkout for high-volume retail-style items
- Working from any screen — you don't need to navigate to a specific "scan" screen first

<Note>
  If scanning doesn't seem to register, confirm your barcode scanner is properly connected/paired, or that you're pressing Enter after manually typing the code.
</Note>
