## The side menu

The left-hand menu is available from anywhere in the app:

| Icon | Name | What it does |
|---|---|---|
| 🔲 | **Tables** | Opens the floor plan — your main screen for dine-in service |
| 🛍️ | **Orders** | Shows all past and active orders, with search and filters |
| 🔔 | **Incoming** | Shows incoming Kiosk or QR orders waiting to be claimed |
| ⏸️ | **Hold** | Access held orders you've paused mid-sale |
| ⚙️ | **Setting** | App and device settings |
| 🗂️ | **Mode** | Switch between Dine In, Takeaway, and other order modes |

## The floor plan

When you open **Tables**, you'll see:

- **Area tabs** at the top — switch between floors or sections (e.g. Ground Floor, Terrace)
- **Table cards** — each shows the table number, seating capacity, and current status:
  - 🔘 **Available** — free and ready for a new guest
  - 🟢 **Occupied** — currently in use, shows guest count and time
  - 🟠 **Reserved** — held for a booking
  - 🔴 **Bill Requested** — guest has asked for the check

## The cart sidebar

Once you open a table, the right-hand panel shows the current bill:

- Items added to this table's order
- Guest count and pax
- **Send to Kitchen**, **Move**, **Settle**, and other action buttons along the top and bottom

<Tip>
  Look for a badge or highlighted row labeled **NEW ORDER** — this shows items that have been added but not yet sent to the kitchen.
</Tip>
