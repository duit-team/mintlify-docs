## Kiosk devices

A Kiosk lets guests place their own orders from a tablet or self-service station.

1. On the Kiosk device, select **Kiosk** during setup.
2. Enter the Master POS device's connection address.
3. Confirm the connection.

Orders placed on a Kiosk appear in the **Incoming** section on the POS for staff to review and claim, or route automatically to the kitchen depending on your configuration.

## Kitchen Display (KDS) devices

A dedicated screen in the kitchen showing live tickets.

1. On the KDS device, select **KDS** during setup.
2. Enter the Master POS device's connection address.
3. Confirm.

See [Using the Kitchen Display](/pos-app/kitchen-display/using-the-kds) for day-to-day usage.

## Checking device status

From the Master device, go to **Settings → Connection & Terminals** to see every connected Kiosk, KDS, and POS device and confirm they're all showing as **Connected**.
