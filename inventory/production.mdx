---
title: "Production"
description: "Turn raw ingredients into a prep item, such as a sauce or cooked rice, at exactly what they cost."
---

A **production batch** uses up the ingredients of a prep item's recipe and puts the finished prep item into stock. Its cost is what went in, so you never type a cost for a prep item.

## Make a batch

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the prep item up first">Give it a [recipe](/inventory/recipes) with kind **Prep item**. The recipe's portions are the batch size (for example 5000 ml for 5 L).</Step>
  <Step title="Go to Inventory → Production">Click **Record batch**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the kitchen and the quantity made">Quantities are in the prep item's stock unit.</Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Only a central kitchen or an outlet can produce. A warehouse stores and ships, so it is not offered.
</Note>

## Cancelling

Cancelling a batch puts the ingredients back and removes the prep item. If some of the batch has already been used or wasted, the cancel is refused, because the prep item cannot be taken out of stock twice.
