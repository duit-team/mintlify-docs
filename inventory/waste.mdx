---
title: "Waste"
description: "Record spoiled, dropped, expired, staff-meal and free items so their cost stops hiding inside your cost of sales."
---

A **waste entry** writes stock off at its real cost. It is recorded straight away — there is no draft.

## Record waste

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Inventory → Waste">Click **Record waste**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the location and the reason">**Spoiled**, **dropped**, **expired**, **staff meal**, **free**, or **other**.</Step>
  <Step title="Add items or dishes">Quantities are in the item's stock unit. For a **dish**, enter portions: its recipe ingredients are written off.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Give staff meals and free items their own reason. Their cost then shows on its own line instead of making your food cost look worse than it is.
</Tip>

## Mistakes

You cannot delete a waste entry. **Cancel** it with a reason: the stock goes back at the cost it left at, and both entries stay on record.
