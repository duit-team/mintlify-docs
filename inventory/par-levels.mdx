title: "Par & reorder levels"

---

An outlet that sells 40 a day needs a different minimum than one that sells 4. **Par & Reorder** lets you set both for each item at each location.

| Level | Meaning |
|-------|---------|
| **Reorder at** | When stock is at or below this, the item needs ordering |
| **Par** | The target to refill up to. It must be at least the reorder level |

A location with no level of its own uses the item's general reorder level from its [stock profile](/retail/stock-profile).

## What to order

Pick a location. Items at or below their reorder level are listed with the quantity needed to get back to par, and grouped by each item's default supplier as **suggested purchase orders**. Nothing is ordered for you: raise a [purchase order](/retail/purchase-orders) from the suggestion.
