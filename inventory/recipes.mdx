title: "Recipes & food cost"
description: "Say what each dish is made of so a sale uses up its ingredients and you can see the food cost."
---

A **recipe** lists the ingredients of one portion of a dish, or of a batch of a prep item. When a dish is sold, those ingredients are taken out of the outlet's stock automatically.

## Write a recipe

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Inventory → Recipes & Food Cost">Click **New recipe**.</Step>
  <Step title="Choose the item and its kind">Pick the product, then **Dish** (sold, never stocked) or **Prep item** (made in-house, stocked).</Step>
  <Step title="Add ingredients">For each one enter the quantity in the ingredient's own unit (grams, millilitres or pieces) and its **yield %**.</Step>
  <Step title="Save">Saving creates a **new version**. Older versions stay on record, read-only.</Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  **Yield %** is what survives trimming or cooking. If 1 kg of raw chicken gives 0.8 kg usable, enter 80: a portion that needs 200 g on the plate uses 250 g of stock.
</Tip>

## Import many recipes at once

Go to **Inventory → Recipes & Food Cost → Import**. Download the template, fill in one row per ingredient, and choose the file.

| Column | Required | Meaning |
|--------|----------|---------|
| **dish** | Yes | Product code or exact name of the dish or prep item |
| **ingredient** | Yes | Product code or exact name of the ingredient |
| **qty** | Yes | Quantity in the ingredient's own unit, per portion |
| **kind** | First time | `dish` or `prep` |
| **portions** | No | Portions made (a prep item: the batch size). Default 1 |
| **yield_pct** | No | What survives trimming or cooking, 1–100. Default 100 |

You see a **preview** of every recipe first: **New**, **New version**, **No change** or **Error** with the row number and reason. Nothing is saved until you click **Import**. A recipe with an error is skipped and the rest are saved. Importing the same file again changes nothing.

<Note>
  A product can only become a dish while nothing depends on its stock: it must not be an ingredient in another recipe, and it must hold no stock.
</Note>

## Food cost

The recipe screen shows the **cost per portion** and the **food-cost %** of the selling price. This is an estimate from current stock cost, for pricing. The real cost of each sale is recorded at the moment of sale, so editing a recipe never changes the cost of past sales.

An asterisk means an ingredient has no cost yet (nothing received), so the figure is an under-estimate.

## Modifiers

A modifier can change what a dish uses:

| Effect | Example |
|--------|---------|
| **Add** | Extra shot adds 18 g of beans |
| **Remove** | No milk removes the milk line |
| **Swap** | Oat milk replaces milk, same quantity |

## Rules

- A recipe cannot contain itself, directly or through another prep item.
- A dish cannot be an ingredient. Use its own ingredients.
- Every ingredient must be a stock-tracked product.
- A dish without a recipe uses up nothing and appears on the [exceptions list](/inventory/usage-and-exceptions).
