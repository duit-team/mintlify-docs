---
title: "Transfers"
description: "Request, send and receive stock between locations, so nothing appears at an outlet that did not arrive."
---

A transfer moves stock in two steps, each confirmed by the people who hold the stock.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Request (optional)">An outlet raises a request for what it needs. Nothing moves.</Step>
  <Step title="Send">The supplying warehouse or kitchen sends it. The stock **leaves the source and is in transit**. The quantity may differ from the request.</Step>
  <Step title="Receive">The outlet enters what actually arrived. Only then does the stock appear at the outlet.</Step>
</Steps>

## Short deliveries

If less arrived than was sent, choose a reason: **damaged**, **lost**, **short shipped** or **other**. The missing quantity is written off as a transit loss at the cost it left at, so no value appears or disappears.

You cannot receive more than was sent.

## Who can do what

- Only people at the **supplying** location can send.
- Only people at the **receiving** location can confirm receipt.
- Someone limited to specific locations sees a transfer if either end is theirs.

A transfer still in transit after three days is flagged on the [exceptions list](/inventory/usage-and-exceptions).

<Note>
  The older single-step [Stock Transfers](/retail/stock-transfers) still exist and move stock instantly. Use this two-step flow when stock needs to be confirmed on arrival.
</Note>
