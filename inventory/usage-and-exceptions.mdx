## Exceptions

A daily list of things to look at:

- **Negative stock** — sold before the delivery was entered. Enter the delivery and the cost of those sales is corrected automatically.
- **Dishes and prep items with no recipe** — they use up nothing until a recipe is written.
- **Transfers stuck in transit** — sent more than three days ago and not yet received.

## Expected vs actual

For each ingredient over a period:

| Column | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Expected** | What the recipes say the sales should have used |
| **Waste** | Recorded waste |
| **Production** | Used making prep batches (expected, not a loss) |
| **Unexplained loss** | A stock count that came in short and nothing above explains |
| **Variance** | (waste + unexplained loss) as a % of expected |

A count that comes in **over** is not a loss and never hides a loss on another item.

<Tip>
  Counts make this report honest. The more often you count, the sooner an over-portioning or unlogged spill shows up.
</Tip>

## Other views

**ABC** ranks items by what they cost you (A = the few making 80% of it). **Days of cover** shows how long stock lasts at the current sales rate. **Near expiry** lists batches that have expired or will within 30 days. **Dead stock** lists items with stock but no movement for 90 days.
