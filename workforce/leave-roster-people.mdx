## Leave

### Request leave

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Workforce → Leave">
    On **My leave**, you see your balance for each leave type this year.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Request leave">
    Pick the type and dates. A half day is possible when the type allows it. The preview shows how many days it uses and what's left, for example "2 days · 6 left after this".
  </Step>
  <Step title="Wait for approval">
    Your manager approves or rejects it. You can cancel it yourself before it starts.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Rest days and public holidays inside a request don't use leave days.

### Approve leave

Managers open **Workforce → Leave → Requests**. Use the list, or the board (drag a request from **Waiting** to **Approved** or **Rejected**). **Calendar** shows who is away each day. You can't decide your own request.

Approved paid leave adds the day's normal hours to the person's timesheet as **Paid leave**, so it flows into the month's approval and export. **Record leave** enters leave for someone and approves it straight away, for example sick leave reported by phone.

### Leave types and balances

**Workforce → Leave types** lists the types from your country preset. Each type sets whether it's paid, whether it needs a document (such as an MC), whether half days are allowed, and how many days people get. That's either a flat number or a number that grows with years of service, using each person's **Joined on** date from **Settings → Users**. Unused days can carry over up to a limit.

On **Leave → Balances**, managers can give someone more days for a year.

## Roster

<Steps>
  <Step title="Plan the week">
    Open **Workforce → Roster**, pick the outlet and week, and add shifts. New shifts are drafts (dashed): staff can't see them yet. **Copy last week** saves time.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish">
    Click **Publish N changes**. Each person gets a message, and the shifts appear on their **Today** page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Follow the day">
    Each shift shows **On time**, **Late**, **Left early** or **No-show** by comparing it with the clock. Late and left early count after the grace minutes in your rules (5 by default).
  </Step>
</Steps>

When someone doesn't turn up, click **Find cover** on their shift. Duit Cloud lists who is free, best first, and who isn't (and why). Pick someone and the shift is published to them.

<Note>
  The roster plans who works when. Pay and overtime still follow the work schedules and your attendance rules.
</Note>

## Staff profiles

Open a person's profile from **Workforce → Timesheets** (their name) or from **Settings → Users**.

| Tab | Contains | Who can edit |
|-----|----------|--------------|
| **Details** | Work details, personal details, contact and emergency contact, residency and work pass | The person edits their own contact and emergency details. Managers with profile access edit the rest. |
| **Bank and statutory** | IC, bank account, EPF, SOCSO, EIS and tax numbers | Only roles with **See and change IC, bank and statutory numbers**. The person sees their own numbers masked (•••5678). |
| **Documents** | Contracts, IC copies, medical certificates (PDF, photos or Word, up to 10 MB), with an optional expiry date | Managers with profile access. Staff can upload their own. |

Documents are private: there is no public link. Every download goes through Duit Cloud, which checks who is asking each time.

**Departments** (**Workforce → Departments**) group people and give each group a head. A department head can switch the timesheet to **Managed by me** to see only their department.

## Outlet opening days

Under **Locations → (your outlet) → Opening days**, set the weekdays the outlet is always closed and dated closures such as renovations. Nobody can clock in at a closed outlet, and the roster and timesheet shade those days.
