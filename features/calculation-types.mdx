---
title: "Calculation Types"
description: "Round bills automatically, and add fixed or percentage charges and discounts to every bill."
---

**Calculation Types** change the amount of every bill automatically. Use them for:

- **Rounding** — for example, round every bill to the nearest 0.05, or always round up to the next 0.10.
- **Charges** — for example, a delivery fee or a packaging charge.
- **Discounts** — for example, a member discount applied to every bill.

You set them up once in the admin. Every POS and Kiosk picks them up on the next sync, and the cashier does nothing — there are no buttons to press. If you have not created any calculation type, bills are exactly as before.

<Note>
  A calculation type is for automatic, bill-wide adjustments. A cashier still applies one-off discounts from the Discount button, and **service charge** stays under [Taxes](/features/tax).
</Note>

## How a bill is worked out

Every bill goes through the same steps, in this order:

<Steps>
  <Step title="Ticket amount">
    Item prices plus modifiers, minus any discount the cashier applied.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Before-tax types">
    Charges and discounts set to **Ticket amount**. They are added to the amount that tax is worked out on.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Tax">
    Your existing taxes, including service charge.
  </Step>
  <Step title="After-tax types">
    Charges and discounts set to **Ticket total**. They are never taxed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Rounding">
    If a rounding type applies, the final total is rounded. This is the amount the customer pays, whatever the payment method.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Every type in the same step works on the same amount — they do not stack on top of each other.

**Example.** A 25.20 ticket with a 10% member discount (before tax), a 5.00 delivery fee (after tax) and rounding to 0.05:

| Line | Amount |
|------|-------:|
| Subtotal | 25.20 |
| Member discount 10% | −2.52 |
| Tax | 3.63 |
| Delivery fee | 5.00 |
| **Total before rounding** | **31.31** |
| Rounding | −0.01 |
| **Total payable** | **31.30** |

The receipt shows each line, and rounding is printed as its own line under the total.

## Create a calculation type

<Steps>
  <Step title="Go to Operations → Calculation Types">
    Click **Calculation Types** under Operations in the left sidebar.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click New type">
    Enter a **Name** (printed on the receipt) and a **Code** (a short unique key used in reports).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose the type">
    Pick **Rounding**, **Charge** or **Discount** and fill in the fields below. The preview on the right shows what the type does to a sample bill.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Map it (optional)">
    Choose where it applies. Leave a list empty for all.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create**. POS devices pick it up the next time they sync or you use **Resync Data**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## Fields

### Charge and discount

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Value type** | **Percentage** of the bill, or a **Fixed amount**. |
| **Rate / Amount** | The percentage (up to 100) or the fixed amount. |
| **Apply on** | **Ticket amount** — before tax, so it is part of the taxed amount. **Ticket total** — after tax, never taxed. |

A fixed discount never takes a bill below zero.

### Rounding

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **Round to** | The step the final total is rounded to, for example `0.05`, `0.10` or `0.50`. |
| **Direction** | **Nearest** (the default) rounds up or down, whichever is closer. **Always up** never lowers the bill. **Always down** never raises it. |

The step must be a whole multiple of the smallest unit of your currency, so it follows your number-of-decimals setting. With 2 decimals you can use `0.05`, `0.10` or `0.50`; `0.005` is only available if your currency uses 3 decimals. A whole-unit currency such as VND can use steps like `500`.

| Bill total | Step | Nearest | Always up | Always down |
|-----------:|-----:|--------:|----------:|------------:|
| 10.12 | 0.05 | 10.10 | 10.15 | 10.10 |
| 10.13 | 0.05 | 10.15 | 10.15 | 10.10 |
| 10.15 | 0.10 | 10.20 | 10.20 | 10.10 |

A refund is rounded the same way as the sale it reverses.

## Where it applies

A type can be mapped to **locations**, **order modes** and **channels** (POS, Kiosk, Online, App and so on).

- **No mapping means all.** A type with nothing selected applies to every location, order mode and channel.
- **Charges and discounts:** every type that matches the bill applies.
- **Rounding:** a bill is rounded only once. If several rounding types match, the most specific one wins — a channel mapping beats a location mapping, which beats an order-mode mapping. A rounding with no mapping is the fallback for everything else.

To treat one place differently, create another type and map it there. For example, keep a general rounding to 0.05 for everyone, and add a second rounding to 0.10 mapped only to your mall outlet.

## Split bills

When a bill is split by items, each guest's bill gets its own charges, discounts and rounding. When a bill is split equally or by amount, the guests pay shares of one bill that already includes its adjustments, so each guest's ticket records its share.

## Reports

Shift reports and business date reports show an **Adjustments & rounding** section when any bill in the period was adjusted:

- Each charge or discount type, with the number of tickets and the total amount.
- **Rounding gain** — bills rounded up, with the count.
- **Rounding loss** — bills rounded down, with the count.
- **Rounding net** — the difference. It is already included in your sales total.

The POS shift summary and the printed shift report show the same section.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Can the cashier turn a charge or discount on or off?">
    No. Calculation types always apply to the bills they match. Use the Discount button for one-off discounts.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I changed a type. When does it reach the POS?">
    On the next sync, or when the cashier uses **Resync Data**. A bill that was already completed keeps the amounts it was charged.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Does it apply to bills made while the POS was offline?">
    Yes. The POS uses the last synced types and records the exact amounts on the bill, so it uploads correctly when the connection returns.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Why was my rounding step rejected?">
    The step must be a whole multiple of the smallest unit of your currency. Check the number of decimals in your settings.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="What happens if I delete a type?">
    It stops applying to new bills. Past bills keep their record of it.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
