## One record per person

Every person in your workspace has **one** record on the **Users** page (**Settings → Users**). How they sign in depends on what you give them:

| Give them | They can sign in to | Typical person |
|-----------|---------------------|----------------|
| **POS PIN** only | The POS, at their outlets | Cashier, waiter, kitchen staff |
| **Email + password** only | The back office (web dashboard) | Accountant, owner who never works a till |
| **Both** | The POS **and** the back office | Manager, supervisor |

<Note>
  Before this change, POS staff were kept in a separate **Employees** list and back-office users in **Users**, so a manager often existed twice. Every employee has been moved into **Users** automatically, keeping the same history (sales, shifts, voids, till entries). Nothing needed to be re-entered.
</Note>

## POS staff fields

| Field | Required | What it does |
|-------|----------|--------------|
| **Name** | Yes | Shown on the POS sign-in screen, receipts and reports. |
| **POS PIN** | For POS sign-in | 4 digits. Must be unique at every outlet the person can sign in at. On edit, leave blank to keep the current PIN. |
| **Roles** | Yes | One or more roles. The person gets **every** permission of **every** role they hold. See [Users & Roles](/setup/users-and-roles). |
| **Locations** | Yes | The outlets where they appear on the POS sign-in screen. "All locations" puts them on every outlet. |
| **POS colour** | No | Highlight colour next to their name on the POS sign-in screen. |
| **Coach** | No | Can be picked as the coach on a plan or class entry. |
| **Active** | No | Turn off to stop them signing in. Their history is kept. |

<Tip>
  Avoid obvious PINs like `1234` or `0000`. The POS identifies the person by PIN alone, which is why a PIN can't be reused at the same outlet.
</Tip>

## Add a POS staff member

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Settings → Users">
    Click **New user**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in name, POS PIN and roles">
    Leave **Email** and **Password** empty for someone who only works the POS. Pick one or more **Roles**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose their outlets">
    Turn off **All locations** and pick the outlets where they work.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    They appear on the POS sign-in screen of those outlets after the POS refreshes its staff list (on start, on **Resync**, or at the next sign-in).
  </Step>
</Steps>

To bring many staff in at once, use **Import POS staff** on the Users page (columns: Name, PIN, Role Name, Outlet Code, Colour, Coach, Active). A row whose PIN is already used at that outlet is imported **without** a PIN — the list then shows *No sign-in — set a POS PIN* next to that person.

## Give a back-office user a POS PIN

Open the person on **Users**, enter a **POS PIN** and save. They now appear on the POS of their outlets with the permissions of all their roles.

## Merge duplicates

If someone existed both as a back-office user and as a POS employee before the change, they now show up twice: once with an email, once marked **POS sign-in only (PIN)**.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Click Find duplicates on the Users page">
    It lists POS-only records whose name matches a record with an email.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Click Merge on the right pair">
    The record with the email is kept. It gains the other record's roles, outlets and (if it had none) PIN. Their sales, shifts and other history move across, and the POS-only record is removed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enter a PIN if asked">
    If the kept record would sign in at more outlets than before, enter the PIN they will use — it has to be checked at all of them.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Warning>
  Only merge when it really is the same person — the history move can't be undone from the screen. A merge is refused while the POS-only record still has an open shift or is signed in on a device: close the shift or sign them out first. POS devices that were offline and still send the old record keep working; their sales land on the kept record.
</Warning>

## Deactivate or delete

Turn **Active** off (or delete the person) to stop them signing in. Their sales, shifts and reports keep their name. A POS that still shows the person drops them at its next staff refresh; if they were signed in, the POS returns to the PIN screen after a **Resync**.

## Frequently asked questions

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Where did the Employees page go?">
    Staff are managed on **Users** now. If you can open Users, the old Employees links take you there. Roles that may manage employees but not users still see the Employees page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Some staff show the role 'POS Full Access (migrated)'">
    Those employees had no role before the change, which meant full access on the POS. The migration gave them this role so nothing changed for them. Replace it with a real role (e.g. Cashier) whenever you're ready.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="Can a POS PIN sign in to the back office?">
    No. A PIN only works on the POS. The back office always needs email + password.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="An employee forgot their PIN">
    Open them on **Users**, type a new **POS PIN** and save. It works after the POS refreshes its staff list.
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>
